How the numbers work

All-play: each completed week, your score is compared with the other nine scores. Expected wins: all-play wins divided by nine. Luck index: actual wins minus expected wins. Schedule swap: your scores are replayed against the opponents from another team's weekly schedule. Ties count as half a win in the all-play expected-wins model.

Only completed fantasy matchups are included. Live and future matchups are excluded until ESPN marks them final.
